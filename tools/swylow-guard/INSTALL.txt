PolyPlus, Swylow edition (2.4.0-swylow.6, Minecraft 26.2, Fabric)
Based on PolyPlus 2.4.0, the version OneClient ships now, with all the Swylow changes on top.

INSTALL
1. Close Minecraft.
2. In the OneClient launcher open your 26.2 instance and open its folder
   (the folder that has "mods" in it).
3. In "mods", delete the old PolyPlus jar (polyplus-2.4.x+26.2.jar or an older swylow one).
4. Put both jars from mod/ in "mods":
   - polyplus-2.4.0-swylow.6+26.2.jar  (the mod)
   - PolyPlus-Swylow-Guard-1.0.0.jar    (tiny helper, see below)
5. Start the game.

Only one PolyPlus jar may be in the mods folder (two of them = crash).

IF THE LAUNCHER RESETS IT
The OneClient launcher can swap our jar back to the official PolyPlus when it
updates the instance. Your menu then looks like the normal one again.
- The guard jar notices this and shows a message in game:
  "PolyPlus was replaced by the launcher". Your menu layout is NOT lost, it is
  saved in config/polyplus/menu-layout.json.
- Fix: close the game, delete the official polyplus jar from "mods", put
  polyplus-2.4.0-swylow.6+26.2.jar back, start again.
- If your launcher has a setting to stop updating mods / the OneClient bundle for
  this instance, turn it off, then it stops happening.
- It never happens in a launcher that doesn't manage the mods for you (for example
  a plain Fabric 26.2 instance with Fabric API, Fabric Language Kotlin, OneConfig
  and this jar).

ESSENTIAL
With Essential installed you'd get two of everything on the title screen.
While the vanilla menu is on, Essential's own title screen buttons are hidden
and only ours show. Essential's Social, Wardrobe, Pictures and Settings can be
added as buttons in the menu editor (Elements > Add element > Essential: ...).
Want Essential's own buttons back? OneConfig > Main Menu (OneClient) > Vanilla
Style > turn off "Hide Essential's Title Screen Buttons".
(Essential also has its own setting: Essential settings > Appearance >
"Essential menu layout" > Off hides its buttons everywhere, also in the pause menu.)

NEW IN swylow.6
- Pan: hold RIGHT click on an empty part of the lobby and drag to slide the
  camera around. Left drag still turns it, scroll zooms.
- Far zoom: zoom much further in and out, and pan far across the scene, so a
  capture from up high (like y 100) can show the whole area.
- Your character on any block: drag it anywhere you can see, also high up. It
  no longer jumps back to where it started.
- Turn your character: hold SHIFT and left drag on your character. A ring shows
  around its feet and you can turn it a full 360 degrees, on any block.
- Right click your character: Hide username, Edit animation. The animation
  panel previews each one on your character when you hover it, click to keep it:
  Wave, Superman, Flying, Meditate, Sit, T-pose, Salute, Arms crossed, Dab,
  Dance, Cheer, Zombie, Think, Spin, Lie down.
- Size: the edit panel has a Size control, or hover an element in the editor
  and use the scroll wheel to make it bigger or smaller.
- Cleaner editor: editing (also in the vanilla style menu) opens a
  OneClient-style side panel instead of switching screens.
- Top bar split up: the logo, the ONECLIENT text and each nav item / icon at
  the top are their own elements now. Move, hide or edit each one.
- Right click a widget (clock, FPS, text...) right on the lobby, no editor
  needed: Edit... lets you change the text, text colour, font, size and add
  your own image.
- Profiles: the editor bar has a "Profiles" button. Save your current layout
  (menu, lobby elements, hidden name and animation) under a name, and load it
  back any time from "Saved profiles", for example after a reset.

NEW IN swylow.5
- Capture any spot: scenes no longer need flat ground, so your own spot on
  hills, roofs or messy terrain can be captured.
- Blur: Customize your lobby > Camera & blur. Turn the background blur on/off
  and set its strength (also in OneConfig > Lobby (OneClient) > 3D Lobby).
- Phase through: same panel. The lobby camera goes through roofs, walls and
  terrain when you look around or zoom out.
- Zoom further out: new scene size "Massive" captures more of the world
  (never more than your render distance).
- Saved angle: in Customize your lobby, aim the camera (drag + scroll), then
  "Save this angle". The lobby always opens on that angle for that scene and
  double click goes back to it. "Use the scene's own angle" clears it.
  Automatic mode still captures and swaps scenes like before.
- The edit (sliders) button on the lobby opens the lobby editor directly.
- Lobby HUD: in the lobby editor click "Add element" for a clock, FPS, RAM,
  CPU, any text, or a button that opens any mod's settings (or a screen, a
  server or a website). Right click an element: Edit... (text, color, icon,
  size, opacity, group), Duplicate, Delete, plus the
  usual Hide/Show, Group/Ungroup and Reset. Drag to move, white corner to
  resize. Widgets, chat, player bar and mod menus button can be moved too.

3D LOBBY (new in swylow.4)
Settings: OneConfig > Lobby (OneClient) > 3D Lobby (all on by default).
- Look around: drag on an empty part of the lobby to turn the camera around
  the scene, scroll to zoom in and out, double click to go back to the normal view.
- Place your character anywhere: hold your character, drag it to any spot you can
  see (also up on hills and ledges) and let go. It stays there.
- Any terrain: scenes on hills, slopes and uneven ground can be captured now,
  and you and your party can stand at different heights.
- Scene size: Normal / Large / Huge. Bigger scenes keep more of the world so
  there is more to look at when you turn around. Only new captures get bigger,
  and never bigger than your render distance.

SETTINGS
OneConfig > Main Menu (OneClient) > Vanilla Style.
Pick "Vanilla" as Main Menu to get the vanilla menu with the OneClient buttons.

SPIN YOUR SKIN
Drag your skin left or right to turn it, let go to give it a spin.
Double click puts it back.

EDIT THE MENU
Vanilla menu: sliders button at the top right > Edit Main Menu > Vanilla Menu.
- Click to select, Shift+click adds, drag on empty space selects with a box.
- Drag to move. Arrow keys move 1 px, Shift+arrows 10 px.
- The white squares resize. Shift keeps the shape, Alt resizes from the middle.
- Ctrl+scroll over a piece scales it.
- Snaps to the grid, to other pieces and to the middle of the screen.
  Hold Ctrl to place freely. Grid on/off and size are in the bar.
- Right click: Hide/Show, Group/Ungroup, Bring to front, Send to back,
  Duplicate, Reset this element, Delete, Properties.
- Alt+click picks one piece inside a group. Ctrl+G groups, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups.
- Double click opens Properties (exact position, size, scale, opacity, text...).
- Elements (in the bar): every piece with an eye to hide/show it (hidden pieces
  stay in the list) and drag rows to change what's on top. "Add element" adds a
  picture, text, the music volume slider, mute when unfocused, music track,
  a custom button (join a server, open a website or open a screen), a panel,
  your skin, Quickplay, a clock, FPS and Essential's buttons.
- Pictures: put PNG, JPG or GIF files in config/polyplus/menu-images
  (Properties has an "Open images folder" button). GIFs show their first frame.
- Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Esc deselects, Esc again saves and closes.
- Preset: Centered, Essential style or OneClient style (replaces your layout),
  plus Copy/Paste layout code to share a layout.
- Drag the bar by its = handle if it's in the way.
Lobby and PolyPlus menu: Edit Main Menu > Lobby / PolyPlus Menu.
  Drag to move (grid snap, Ctrl = free), drag the white corner to scale,
  right click to hide/show, group/ungroup or reset. The lobby top bar
  (logo, Play, Wardrobe, ... and your account) is now separate pieces in one
  group: right click > Ungroup to move them one by one.

GO BACK TO NORMAL
Delete both jars and put the official PolyPlus jar back (or let the launcher reinstall it).
